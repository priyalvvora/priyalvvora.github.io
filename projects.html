Marketplace Analytics · Growth Strategy

DoorDash — Marketplace Review & Growth Diagnosis

Analyzed a month of DoorDash orders across customers, merchants, and Dashers to find what limits order frequency and retention. Cleaned the data in Excel and built pivots, weekly-trend, and retention views, then delivered a six-slide executive readout: 3 in 4 customers order once and absorb 69% of discounts, 7 of 62 restaurants carry 53% of volume, and 77% of Dashers last a single week. Each finding pairs with a prioritized move.
